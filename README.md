# PlayGround

## HELLFIRE PROTOCOL

A short, self-contained Doom-inspired arena shooter. A cult has opened a gate; you have a pyro-carbine and twelve hostiles between you and the exit. Cultists hurl fireballs, cinder brutes throw three-shot spreads, and every wave hits harder.

- **Desktop:** `WASD` move, mouse aim, click or `Space` fire, `Shift` sprint, `Esc` pause.
- **Mobile:** left stick moves, drag the right side to look, tap **FIRE** to shoot.
- **Goal:** banish all 12 enemies across three waves. Soul embers dropped by enemies restore health.
- No downloads, no external art or audio. The world, sprites, textures, and sound effects are generated in the browser.
